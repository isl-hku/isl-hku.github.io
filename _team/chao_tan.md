### Bio

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at The University of Hong Kong (HKU), advised by [Prof. Edmund Y. Lam](https://web.ece.hku.hk/~elam/). I joined HKU in September 2026. Prior to joining HKU, I received my M.S. degree in Bio and Brain Engineering from KAIST under the supervision of [Prof. Mooseok Jang](https://mooolab.kaist.ac.kr/index.html), and my B.Eng. degree from Sichuan University.

My research centers on perception: how imaging systems can sense and interpret their environments more efficiently and rapidly. I am interested in perception across spatial scales, from macroscopic scenes to microscopic structures, and across representations from 2D to 3D. In the long term, I aspire to develop imaging instruments that can capture and recreate meaningful memories as rich, multidimensional experiences beyond conventional photographs and videos.

<hr>

### Research Interests

- Event-based vision and machine perception
- Computational photography
- Computational microscopy
- Fourier ptychography and diffraction tomography
- Physics-informed imaging and 3D reconstruction
